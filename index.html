Frequently Asked Questions
How do I submit my daily challenge? expand_more
You submit by pushing code to your public GitHub repository and posting your key learnings on LinkedIn. Paste both links into your Student Dashboard before the daily lock timer expires.
What happens if I miss a deadline? expand_more
Missing a daily deadline breaks your active streak and locks points for that specific day. You can still complete missed days in History mode for partial credit.
How is leaderboard ranking calculated? expand_more
Points are awarded based on completion speed (+100 max per day), code submission quality, and active streak multipliers. The top 10 participants are highlighted on the live leaderboard.
