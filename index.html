Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the challenge
How does daily submission work? expand_more
Submit your GitHub commit or repository link alongside your LinkedIn post link in the student portal before 12:00 AM IST every day.
What happens if I miss a daily deadline? expand_more
Missing the 12:00 AM IST cutoff resets your active streak to 0. You can still complete remaining days for points, but continuous streak badges require daily punctuality.
How are leaderboard points calculated? expand_more
Each verified daily task grants 100 points. Bonus points are awarded for consistency milestones and top-ranked community posts.
Who can participate in ABTalks Tech Challenge? expand_more
The challenge is open to all software engineering students, developers, and tech enthusiasts looking to build a solid proof-of-work portfolio.
